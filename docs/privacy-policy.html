Billu and Friends

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Billu and Friends collects no personal information. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app or on your phone is sent anywhere.

About this policy

This policy explains how the Billu and Friends app for Android ("the app") handles information. The app is published on Google Play by the developer named on its Google Play listing ("we").

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our behalf, sell or share any personal or sensitive information, and it does not ask for any. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send information off your device.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party code that collects data.

What the app does on your device

To show a companion that lives on top of your screen, the app does the following, entirely on your device:

  • Display over other apps. The app draws the companion, and the ball of wool it plays with, in small windows above other apps. It cannot see what those apps show.
  • Touches. When you touch the screen outside the companion, Android tells the app only that a touch happened, not where it was or what you touched. The companion may look up in response. Nothing about the touch is kept.
  • The on-screen keyboard. To let the companion hop onto your keyboard, the app works out where the top of the keyboard is. It cannot see or record what you type.
  • A notification. Android requires a notification while the app runs in the background. It shows the companion's name and opens the app when tapped.

Settings stored on your device

The app saves your choices on your device: whether the companion is shown, its size, the name you give it and which behaviours are switched on. These settings never leave your device through the app. If you use Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include them in that backup; that backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access it.

To delete these settings, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
